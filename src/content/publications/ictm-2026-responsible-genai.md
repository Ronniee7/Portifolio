# Abstract

This paper explores a governance-integrated framework for responsible generative AI in customer service, with particular attention to bias mitigation, explainability, privacy, and oversight. The framework integrates retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector storage, PII protection, natural language inference (NLI), toxicity evaluation, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) governance.
