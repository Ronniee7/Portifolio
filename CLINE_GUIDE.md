## Battery Energy Storage System Control Parameter Optimization

Supervisor: Dr. Xudong Gao.

Institution: NUIST.

Research focus:

Control parameter optimization for battery energy storage systems.

### Problem

A DC–DC converter must operate effectively under changes in battery voltage and power demand.

The research explores how suitable controller parameters can be selected so that system performance remains satisfactory under changing operating conditions.

### Initial methodology

1. Develop a simplified MATLAB/Simulink model.
2. Implement baseline PI control.
3. Identify suitable performance indicators.
4. Study controller behavior under different operating conditions.
5. Investigate optimization of controller parameters.
6. Compare optimized controller settings against baseline PI control.
7. Evaluate performance under battery voltage and power demand variations.

### Possible evaluation indicators

- Rise time.
- Settling time.
- Overshoot.
- Steady-state error.
- Voltage regulation.
- Current response.
- Control effort.
- Robustness across operating conditions.

These are research directions and candidate metrics, not completed results.
